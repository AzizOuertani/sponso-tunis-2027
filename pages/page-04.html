Event Series Prospectus Fork it! Tunis on April 18, 2026 Fork it! Tunis 2026 took place on April 18 at City of Culture in Tunis, bringing together the local and international tech community for a full day of practical, experience-driven talks. This second full-day edition in Tunisia gathered more than 170 attendees: developers, designers, Product Managers, students, and founders. Built on a “no bullshit” approach, the event focused on real-world insights from production, covering topics such as architecture, AI, Web3, and modern development practices. With 8 talks from 12 speakers, the conference combined high-quality content with strong networking moments throughout the day. The event was supported by multiple sponsors and partners, contributing to its overall success and impact. Big thanks to Camel Studio, TinkerView, baksla.sh, Ysely, and partners CWCloud, 9Rayty, GDG Issam, The Pulse Digital Hub, Inceptum Junior Entreprise.
