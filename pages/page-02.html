Community's mission is to share software engineering knowledge through events around the world, connecting practitioners and enthusiasts in an environment focused on the honest exchange of practical insights and fostering a network built on real-world experience and practical applications. About Fork it! Community Events for people in tech around the world to share real-life experiences, supported by a community built on honest, practical feedback.
