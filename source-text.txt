Event Series Prospectus
Fork it! Community's mission is to share software engineering knowledge through events around the world, 
connecting practitioners and enthusiasts in an environment focused on the honest exchange of practical insights and 
fostering a network built on real-world experience and practical applications.
About Fork it! Community
Events for people in tech around the world to share 
real-life experiences, supported by a community built 
on honest, practical feedback.
Each edition is shaped around a central theme and 
explores a wide range of topics, including technical 
project management, UI/UX design, front-end and 
back-end development, DevOps, security, and more. 
The goal is to provide concrete insights rooted in real 
projects and practical experience, offering lasting value 
to every participant.
Fork it! continues to grow worldwide
Fork it! events have brought together vibrant communities across 7 countries 
and 11 cities, including Brussels, Paris, Rouen, Montpellier, Casablanca, 
Tunis, Nabeul, Hanoi, Da Nang, Bangkok, and Kuala Lumpur. More editions 
are planned, with new cities coming soon.

Fork it! Since the Beginning
Since June 2024, Fork it! Community has united tech 
enthusiasts worldwide, expanding its network for sharing 
experiences and fostering collaboration to drive innovation.
Fork it! is also a human story. It began with a group of tech 
professionals from Normandy who, through Fork it!, found 
their voice on stage and went on to share their expertise at 
conferences around the world. Special recognition goes to: 
Ivan Dalmet, Yoann Fleury, Hugo Pérard, Nicolas Torion, 
Marie Douet and many others.

Fork it! Tunis on April 18, 2026
Fork it! Tunis 2026 took place on April 18 at City of Culture in Tunis, 
bringing together the local and international tech community for a full day 
of practical, experience-driven talks. This second full-day edition in Tunisia 
gathered more than 170 attendees: developers, designers, Product 
Managers, students, and founders.
Built on a no bullshit
 approach, the event focused on real-world insights 
from production, covering topics such as architecture, AI, Web3, and modern 
development practices. With 8 talks from 12 speakers, the conference 
combined high-quality content with strong networking moments throughout 
the day. The event was supported by multiple sponsors and partners, 
contributing to its overall success and impact.
Big thanks to Camel Studio, TinkerView, baksla.sh, Ysely, and partners 
CWCloud, 9Rayty, GDG Issam, The Pulse Digital Hub, Inceptum Junior 
Entreprise.
